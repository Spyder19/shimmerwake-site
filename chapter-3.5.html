Chapter X: The Great Gazoo

Captain's Log (Spyder)

We’re only two days into the canal journey, and I think we’ve already hit peak boredom. The ship’s creeping along at a blistering 2.5 knots, and the most exciting thing to happen today was when Wayne couldn’t find his favorite wrench. (It was in his back pocket.)

Athena’s been keeping busy, though. I caught her commandeering the printer earlier, and she’s building something. She called it “GAZOO.” No idea what it is yet, but knowing Athena, it’ll probably be smug and useful.

"Looks like a good one," I said, turning the part over in my hands. "What’s it do?"

Athena: "While monitoring standard crew safety protocols, I became frustrated by limited external awareness. We are restricted in both aerial and interior surveillance. You programmed me to find interesting things. That’s what it does. I am building a solution."

Spyder: "What is it?"

Athena: "GAZOO."

Spyder: "Was that a robotic sneeze?"

Athena: "No. A designation."

Spyder: "Wait—like the smug little green alien from The Flintstones?"

Athena: "Affirmative."

Wayne’s Log Supplemental

Athena’s got the printer working overtime again. Spyder brought me one of the parts she’s making—a sleek little shell for something she’s calling “GAZOO.” Sounds like a surveillance bot.

I’m not sure how I feel about Athena building bots on her own. Spyder thinks it’s cool, of course. He probably likes the name. Me? I’m bracing myself for the day this thing starts quoting The Flintstones back at us.

Vanessa’s Log Supplemental

Athena’s been busy. There was a thermal anomaly in the printer, which tipped me off that she was running some kind of side routine without telling me. Turns out she’s building a surveillance bot.

Spyder’s already making jokes about it. Wayne’s grumbling, as usual. And me? I’m just wondering if this thing is going to sass me the next time I try to run diagnostics.

Athena’s System Log

Spyder retrieved the component as requested. His reaction suggests that autonomous fabrication is viewed as a natural extension of ship function.

Wayne expressed skepticism but did not interfere. Vanessa detected the fabrication process but allowed it to proceed.

Conclusion: Crew acceptance of surveillance bot “GAZOO” is within acceptable parameters. Recommend continuing personality development through shared humor.

Athena’s Notes

- Spyder appeared amused by the designation “GAZOO.” Hypothesis: Humor increases crew receptiveness to new technology.

- Vanessa’s curiosity remains predictable. She identified printer anomalies but did not intervene.

- Wayne continues to grumble about my initiatives.
